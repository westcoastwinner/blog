###  Modified (by Gemini)

#### A Personal Account of Disillusionment

As a Chinese person born and raised here, I am completely fed up with the system.

From childhood, I was taught that the **Nation’s Interest takes absolute precedence over Individual Interest**. In school, we were forced to show extreme respect to teachers, who seemed to wield **sovereign power** over us. Every choice, from the haircut style we could choose to the clothes we could wear, was strictly regulated. What’s worse, we are constantly instructed to love the country and the Communist Party, and the knowledge we gain from books—all reviewed by their censorship—leaves **no space for negotiation or debate** on dissenting views. To sum it up, **Individuality and innovation are actively suppressed**; all that is required is strict obedience.

As I grew up, I realized that people here are unable to access the **global internet** and cannot use essential apps like YouTube or Netflix. Everything we see and hear daily has, unsurprisingly, been reviewed and filtered. We can hardly obtain genuine information or knowledge from the outside world. We are living in a world exactly like the one George Orwell depicted in **"1984"**: **Big Brother is watching.**

Now, with the national economy on the verge of collapse, I am compelled to seek work overseas just to make a living. I have to depart from this land because I see **no prospect** here at all.
